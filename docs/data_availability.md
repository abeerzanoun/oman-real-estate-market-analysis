## Evidence and interpretation

- **2024–2025:** [NCSI October 2025 bulletin](https://api.ncsi.gov.om/uploads/pdfs/publicationpdf1761108337.pdf), printed page 33/table 29: annual 2024, July–September 2025 and January–September comparisons. Page 32/table 28: Q2 2024 and Q1/Q2 2025 indices, category detail and governorates within residential land.
- **2025–2026:** [NCSI September 2026 bulletin](https://api.ncsi.gov.om/uploads/pdfs/monthly_statistical_bulletin__september_2026_1789899633.pdf), printed pages 32–33/tables 28–29: annual 2025, June–August 2026, January–August comparisons and Q1/Q2 2026 index columns. 2026 remains partial.
- **2020–2021 leads:** [NCSI June 2021 bulletin, legacy URL](https://www.ncsi.gov.om/Elibrary/LibraryContentDoc/bar_Monthly%20Statistical%20Bulletin%20June%202021_621015f4-dd38-4bc3-87b4-73bb440433ca.pdf) is indexed with 2020 comparison columns but the web fetch returned 404. [MOHUP annual report 2021](https://cms.mohup.gov.om/uploads/altqryr_alihsayy_alsnwy_2021_d58feaee08.pdf) is indexed with multi-year ministry indicators, not yet inspected. [NCSI 2020 news](https://www.ncsi.gov.om/News/Pages/NewsCT_20200727082231608.aspx) is an official contextual lead; its indexed prose contains an apparent direction/percentage inconsistency, so no observations are extracted from it.
- **2021 regional lead:** [MOHUP Q1 2021 bulletin](https://cms.mohup.gov.om/uploads/alnshrt_aleqaryt_alrbe_alawl_2021_c19fd48bb9.pdf) has indexed regional tables; web retrieval timed out. It is not an accepted regional dataset.
- **2022 regional lead:** [MOHUP Q1 2022 bulletin](https://cms.mohup.gov.om/uploads/alnshrt_aleqaryt_alrbe_alawl_2022m_2380e04113.pdf); exact periods, units and table scope await retrieval. National historical activity is also indicated by an indexed [NCSI January 2023 bulletin](https://www.ncsi.gov.om/Elibrary/LibraryContentDoc/bar_Monthly%20Statistical%20Bulletin%20Jan%202023_473c6f77-69af-425d-9631-439fb3948ffd.pdf); legacy URL does not currently resolve through the web tool.
- **2023 index lead:** [NCSI February 2024 bulletin, legacy URL](https://www.ncsi.gov.om/Elibrary/LibraryContentDoc/bar_Monthly%20Statistical%20Bulletin_fef39be2-c183-4862-a0d3-21ff3e821734.pdf) is indexed with a residential price-index table. Legacy URL returned 404; migrated API-host candidate remains unverified.
- **2024 land-sale lead:** [MOHUP transaction page](https://mohup.gov.om/ar/open-data/data/Realestate%20Transaction), official search index lists monthly releases, but direct web retrieval timed out.
- **2025 regional lead:** [National portal brokerage-registration listing](https://opendata.gov.om/en/datasets/274cbe74-9a28-413a-8a11-7034fc1753cc) is indexed with regional 2025 datasets. Related-dataset text may appear in results; the dataset represented by this exact page, its downloadable resources and specific geography require inspection. No full-market coverage is inferred.

V means the stated slice exists online. It does **not** mean all months/quarters exist, publication dates are confirmed, original checksums are available, or the records have been processed. Years marked ? remain discovery gaps rather than proven nonavailability. Land-use/building-permit data and real-estate GDP are context indicators, not transaction measures.
